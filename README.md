# Resona

### From emergency-room conversation to a clear, reviewable record

Resona is an AI-powered emergency documentation platform that listens to fast-moving care conversations and turns them into a structured clinical timeline. It helps care teams capture what happened, when it happened, who said it, and what evidence supports each documented event—without interrupting the flow of care.

Built for an AI for Health hackathon, Resona focuses on one of emergency medicine’s most difficult documentation problems: important details are often spoken once, amid alarms, overlapping voices, time pressure, and rapidly changing patient conditions.

## The experience

1. **Capture the encounter** using a microphone, an existing audio file, or a built-in demonstration case.
2. **Follow the transcript live** while Resona identifies meaningful clinical moments.
3. **Review the timeline** alongside the exact words and timestamps that support every event.
4. **Correct or add details** by editing the transcript, updating timeline items, or recording a voice addendum.
5. **Generate the final report** with a visual event map, a concise handoff summary, and case-specific feedback.
6. **Return to saved cases** through a patient library of previous timelines and reports.

## Key features

- **Live emergency transcription** with recording controls, an elapsed-time stopwatch, audio-level feedback, and automatic final refinement.
- **Speaker-aware conversations** that separate voices and recognize spoken roles such as Doctor, Nurse, and Paramedic.
- **Evidence-linked timeline** connecting every documented event to its original transcript quote and timestamp.
- **Clinical event recognition** for observations, vital signs, medications, procedures, IV access, blood products, imaging, equipment, and team communication.
- **Human review tools** to keep, edit, remove, or manually add timeline items before a report is created.
- **Voice corrections** for quickly adding an omitted detail without rebuilding the session.
- **Visual event map** that makes the sequence of care easy to understand at a glance.
- **Case-specific feedback** highlighting what went well, what could improve, and what may have been missed.
- **Handoff summary** that condenses the documented encounter into a practical paragraph with one-click copying.
- **Patient library** for reopening saved cases and their complete timelines.
- **Flexible exports** for sharing structured data or a polished printable report.

## Designed for trust

Resona keeps the original conversation at the center of the workflow. It does not treat a request as proof that an intervention was completed. Events remain connected to the words that produced them, speakers and timeline items can be corrected, and reports stay editable before use.

Audio, transcripts, and records are processed locally. No paid inference service is required, and sensitive recordings are not sent to a hosted AI provider.

## Why it matters

Emergency teams should not have to choose between caring for the patient and preserving an accurate record. Resona creates a shared, chronological view of the encounter so teams can review decisions, improve handoffs, identify documentation gaps, and spend less time reconstructing events after the fact.

The result is documentation that is faster to create, easier to verify, and more useful for care-team communication, education, and quality improvement.
